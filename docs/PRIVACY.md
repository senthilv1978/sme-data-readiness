# Privacy Notice (MVP)

## What is stored

The app stores the assessment profile, selected answers, current question and action statuses in the browser’s local storage under `sme-readiness-assessment-v1`. Optional free-text fields are limited in length. The app does not intentionally transmit this information to a server, use an account, or include analytics.

## Where it is stored and who can access it

Data stays in the browser profile on the device. People with access to the same browser profile/device may be able to view it. Browser cleanup, private browsing behavior, device failures or storage restrictions may remove or prevent saving the data.

## Export and deletion

The user can download a JSON export, print or save a PDF, and delete the app’s current local assessment. These operations do not erase copies already downloaded, printed, backed up, or saved in another browser profile.

## Data minimization

Do not enter credentials, customer records, personal data, bank details, or other sensitive content. The product does not need these to calculate results.

## Deployment considerations

The host serving the website may process ordinary web request metadata according to its own privacy policy. Before deployment, publish an organization-specific privacy contact and legal notice if required. A backend, analytics, sharing links or integrations would require a new privacy and security review, clear retention terms, and appropriate consent and controls.
